About

Hi, I'm Cecilia — nice to meet you.

A behavioral therapist turned UX researcher, currently based in Seattle and pursuing my M.S. in Human-Centered Design & Engineering at the University of Washington.

A little about me

Why psychology

I'm Cecilia. I studied psychology because I've always been fascinated by people — what shapes us, what gets in our way, and how much of our behavior is shaped by the environments around us.

Since then

I've done a little bit of everything: UX research in healthcare, direct service with neurodivergent youth and families, food-access work, bartending, yoga and movement, and a lot of wandering around cities looking for good food, music, art, and people.

What ties it together

Those things probably look unrelated on paper. To me, they're not. I care a lot about people, access, community, and making everyday life feel a little more possible for everyone. I'm happiest somewhere between research and making — talking to people, noticing patterns, trying things, and figuring out how to turn an idea into something real.

Right now

I'm currently studying Human Centered Design & Engineering at UW, but I'm less interested in fitting myself into one job title than I am in following good questions wherever they lead.

A few other things about me: I'm bilingual in English and Korean, and this year I've been trying to get more comfortable trying new things in public — this website is one of them.
